Glad to meet you here! I am Zhoujun (pronounced similar to Jorge). I am now a Ph.D. student at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Zhiting Hu.

Prior to this, I received my B.E. and M.E. degrees in Computer Science (IEEE class) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I also spent great times in HKUNLP advised by Prof. Tao Yu and Microsoft Research Asia with Haoyu Dong.
